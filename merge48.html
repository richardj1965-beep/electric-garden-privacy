merge48 — Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026 · Electric Garden Apps

merge48 collects nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, and no data leaves your device. The app does not request the Android internet permission, so it has no ability to send anything anywhere.

What the app stores

  • Your game: the board, score, move and merge counters, undo history and next-tile state — so a game in progress survives closing the app.
  • Best scores, per board size.
  • Your settings: theme, board size, win target, and the sound and haptics switches.

All of this lives in the app's own private storage on your device. It is not backed up to us, because there is nowhere for it to go. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Exports are yours

The app can save a game to a file and read one back. That happens only when you choose it, through Android's own file picker, and the file goes wherever you point it. We never see it.

No third parties

There are no analytics, advertising, attribution or crash-reporting services in the app. The release build contains no software development kit from any advertising or data company. No data is sold, shared, or transferred to anyone.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, children included. It contains no ads, no purchases and no user-generated content.

Permissions

The only permission in the manifest is a signature-level broadcast permission Android generates for the app itself. There is no internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permission.

Changes

If a future version ever changes any of the above, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date at the top will change.
